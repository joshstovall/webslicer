# Printing orientation

Choose **More model tools → Find printing orientation** for the selected object. With **Transform entire assembly** enabled, linked parts are evaluated together and receive one rigid world rotation. Hidden linked parts follow the transformation but do not affect the search. Choose a priority, select **Find angles**, and compare the recommended angle with alternatives in the interactive preview. **Apply and arrange** stages the chosen angle and arranges the active plate with the saved arrangement preferences. It commits the entire result as one undoable edit.

Cancel during search, review or arrangement preserves the original project. If arrangement fails, its existing retry/cancel/keep choices apply; keeping explicitly accepts the rotated geometry for manual placement. The final slice still validates geometry and bounds. Local triangles, material assignments, surface/support paint, modifiers, scales and assembly membership are preserved; only world position and rotation change.

## Estimates and search

This is an original implementation using the existing Manifold and Three.js dependencies. The selected visible closed solids are unioned for analysis so internal material boundaries and buried overlapping faces do not produce spurious support demand. Invalid/open solids require repair. The algorithm considers the current up direction, six principal directions, up to 32 area-weighted surface-normal groups and 24 distributed sphere directions. Surface groups use the normal of their largest face so a real face can contact the bed. A quarter turn around the vertical is allowed when needed for rectangular bed fit. A failed finite search does not prove there is no printable orientation.

Every candidate uses full union geometry for build-volume bounds and bed contact. Bed contact sums downward planar faces at the lowest plane; it is not an adhesion/strength prediction. Support estimates use 4,096 deterministic, area-stratified surface samples and a triangle bounding-volume tree. Rays extend from overhangs to the nearest lower model surface or the grounded bed. The area-weighted column volume therefore excludes intervening solids instead of extending every overhang to the bed. It remains an estimate: finite sampling can miss small features, and support density, interfaces, tree routes, bridges, support paint/modifiers, neighboring unselected objects and filament changes are not simulated. The displayed solid-column space is not filament consumption.

The overhang test follows the pinned slicing engine: downward normal component at least `sin(supportAngle)`. Balanced ranking combines estimated support volume normalized by solid volume, overhang area, planar bed contact relative to the bounding footprint and normalized height. Other priorities change those weights; they do not claim a global optimum. The current grounded angle is measured with the same estimator for comparison. Review proposed angles for appearance and directional strength. This workflow follows the general candidate/review approach described in the [official OrcaSlicer orientation documentation](https://github.com/orcaslicer/orcaslicer/wiki/prepare_auto_orient); it does not include OrcaSlicer implementation code.

The bed-fit screen includes saved edge clearance or adhesion margin, whichever is larger. Reserved areas and other objects are checked during the subsequent outline arrangement. Future support envelopes, fixed purge towers and sequential-print head clearance are not incorporated into this search. These remain separate broader-goal work.

## Worker lifecycle and bounds

The search loads locally in a disposable worker. Cancellation, completion, load failure, unreadable messages and a three-minute deadline terminate it. Native solids are disposed before a result is returned. Limits are 200 visible parts, 500,000 input triangles and 750,000 triangles after union. The input limit is checked before creating the transformed copy on the main thread. Complex native unions and acceleration structures can still consume substantial memory; large-model and real-device performance remain part of the broader audit.
